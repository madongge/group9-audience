从一个任务，找到值得试的设计Turn a job into a design worth trying

一条蛋白质链像一串珠子。氨基酸是珠子，排列会影响整条链折成什么形状。A protein chain is like a string of beads. The beads are amino acids. Their order helps determine how the chain folds.

  1. 定任务。我们希望它做什么？Set the job. What should it do?
  2. 找形状。模型从已有结构中学规律，提出可能合适的形状。Propose a shape. A model learns from known structures and suggests possible shapes.
  3. 排珠子。另一种工具寻找可能折出这个形状的氨基酸排列。Find a sequence. Another tool suggests an order of amino acids that might fold into that shape.
  4. 做检验。先用计算检查设计，再用实验看看它能否完成任务。Check and test. Use computer predictions to screen the design, then test what it does in the lab.

以 RFdiffusion 类工作流程为例。David Baker 因计算蛋白质设计获得 2024 年诺贝尔化学奖的一半。This is a simplified RFdiffusion-style workflow. David Baker received half of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for computational protein design.

每一步都要有相应证据。结合实验回答的是「抓得住吗」。能否阻断信号、在人体中是否安全有效，需要其他研究。Each step needs its own evidence. A binding experiment asks whether the protein catches its target. Other studies examine signal blocking, safety and benefit in people.

调节免疫信号Change an immune signal

一些皮肤和肠道炎症疾病也有针对免疫信号的蛋白质药物。已有治疗让研究人员知道，干预明确的疾病环节可以有用。Protein medicines also target immune signals in some inflammatory skin and bowel diseases. Existing treatments show how acting on one part of a disease can help.

帮助免疫系统认出威胁Help the immune system recognise a threat

获批用于预防新冠的 SKYCovione 疫苗，使用计算设计的蛋白质颗粒来展示病毒特征。可以把颗粒想成微小的展示架。The authorised COVID-19 vaccine SKYCovione uses computationally designed protein particles to display features of the virus. Think of the particles as tiny display stands.

对一些类风湿关节炎患者，早晨拧瓶盖、拿杯子或扣衣服都可能费劲。为什么会这样？For some people with rheumatoid arthritis, opening a bottle, holding a cup or fastening a button can be difficult in the morning. Why?

  1. 01

    关节里发生了什么？What happens in the joint?

    免疫系统通常帮我们抵御感染。在类风湿关节炎中，免疫反应会让关节的内衬持续发炎，关节因此可能肿、痛、僵硬。The immune system usually protects us from infection. In rheumatoid arthritis, immune activity keeps the joint lining inflamed. The joint can swell, hurt and feel stiff.

  2. 02

    细胞怎么收到消息？How do cells receive a message?

    细胞靠分子传递消息。TNF 是其中一种信号。它碰到细胞表面的接收器时，可以启动炎症反应。Cells send messages using molecules. TNF is one of those signals. When it reaches a receiver on a cell’s surface, it can trigger an inflammatory response.

  3. 03

    能拦下这条消息吗？Can we intercept the message?

    阿达木单抗是一种已经用于治疗的蛋白质药物。它能抓住 TNF，阻断这条信号。下面用简化模型看看，这需要满足什么条件。Adalimumab is an existing protein medicine. It binds TNF and blocks its signal. Use the simplified model below to see what blocking requires.
